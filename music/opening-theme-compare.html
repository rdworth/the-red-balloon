What's different
  • Same notes, same tempo and the same cue times, started on the same clock, so the two stay in lockstep.
  • The right side swaps the synth voices for recordings from the free FluidR3 General MIDI soundfont: accordion, plucked upright bass, and marimba for the music-box sparkles.
  • The pigeon's wing flaps and Pascal's footsteps are the same synthesized sounds on both sides.
  • Drag the balloon: all the way left is the synth alone, all the way right is the recordings alone, the middle plays both.

The animation can use await OpeningThemeSampled.load() and then OpeningThemeSampled.play({ duration }), exactly like OpeningTheme.
