The Red Balloon · Scene 1, alternate take · 20 seconds

The balloon drifts over Paris, in marker

The same opening, redrawn in shaky felt-tip marker in the spirit of Bill Plympton. The city is built in 3D, so the camera can circle the balloon in the clouds, swoop over the chimneys, duck under the laundry and crane down the lamppost to Pascal's shoes. Every drawing boils, 12 a second, to the same little waltz.
